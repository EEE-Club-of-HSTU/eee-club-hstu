How the EEE Club of HSTU handles form submissions, analytics consent and student data.

Last updated · Nov 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by the EEE Club of HSTU, the student engineering organisation of the Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science & Technology University (HSTU), Dinajpur, Bangladesh. Every enrolled EEE student at HSTU is automatically a club member; you do not need to create an account to browse this site.

2. Information you choose to share

We only collect personal details that you deliberately submit through our public forms:

  • Alumni Association registration: full name, academic session, graduation year, email address, optional student ID, phone number, current role, organisation, city, LinkedIn URL, mentoring interests and message.
  • Contact form: name, email address, topic, optional organisation and message.
  • Pulse newsletter: email address for monthly club updates.

3. How data is stored and used

Form submissions are transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) for our club committee to review alumni registrations, reply to messages and send club updates. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information with third parties.

4. Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics (with IP anonymization enabled) only if you explicitly click Accept on our consent banner. If you click Decline or make no choice, Google Analytics scripts are not loaded and no analytics requests are sent. You can change your preference at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer.

5. Local browser storage

Your browser stores two small preferences locally on your device: your analytics consent choice and whether you dismissed the install prompt. This website also registers a service worker so core pages remain readable offline.

6. Your rights & contact

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your alumni directory entry, contact message or newsletter subscription at any time by emailing eeeclub@hstu.ac.bd or via our Contact page.
