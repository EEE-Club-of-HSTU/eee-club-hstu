answered.

Who is a member of the EEE Club?
Every enrolled student of the Department of EEE at HSTU, from Level 1 to Level 4 and postgraduates, is a member automatically. There’s nothing to sign up for. Students from other departments can attend selected events as guests.
Is there a membership fee?
No. Membership is free and automatic. A few events, such as Volt Rush, have a small team registration fee.
I’ve graduated. How do I stay involved?
Register with the EEE Alumni Association. Once the committee verifies you, we’ll invite you to mentor, speak and meet current students.
Do I need prior experience?
None at all. Every wing runs a beginner track in the first month, and most members built their first circuit with us.
How can a company sponsor an event?
Choose “Sponsorship” in the form above. We’ll send our sponsorship deck with packages for Volt Rush, workshops and Pulse magazine.
How do I verify my certificate?
Enter the ID printed on your certificate on the Verify page, or scan its QR code.
