(04) — FAQ

Alumni,
answered.

Who can register with the EEE Alumni Association?
Any graduate of the Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering at HSTU (B.Sc. or M.Sc.). If you are still an enrolled student, you are already a member of the EEE Club automatically.
How long does verification take?
The executive & alumni committee reviews new registrations weekly. Expect a confirmation email within seven days once your batch details are checked.
Is there any alumni registration fee?
No. Registering with the directory is free. Optional lab support or event sponsorships can be coordinated directly with the committee if you choose.
How do I update my role or email later?
Send us a quick note on the Contact page with the “Alumni” topic selected and we’ll update your record.
